## Rules for every prompt

- Palette by hex, always repeated: ledger cream `#F4EEE1`, cotton `#FBF8F0`, kraft `#EAE2D0`, ink `#1D2421`, stamp vermilion `#BC3A22`, leaf `#2F6B4F`, haldi `#E8A317`. Bright, light, premium daylight grade. No dark or neon looks.
- **No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks or faces** in any video. Ruled lines stay blank; the stamp impression is an abstract ring and bars, never letters.
- 16:9, 1920x1080 or higher, 24 or 30 fps, 8 s, no audio, slow steady camera, smooth easing.
- The three landing clips run problem, transformation, resolved. **Clip 3 is a seamless loop** (first frame equals last frame).

## Landing clips

**Landing 1: the problem (8 s).** Overhead, slightly angled, slow push-in. A scuffed kraft shoebox on a worn wooden shop counter spills a heap of crumpled paper bills and receipts; a brass weighing scale, a ballpoint pen and a steel ink pad sit to one side. Soft warm morning window light from the left, ledger-cream `#F4EEE1` and kraft `#EAE2D0` tones, ink `#1D2421` shadows, one small vermilion `#BC3A22` ink pad as the only saturated colour. Paper has no readable writing, only abstract scribbles. No people. Calm, slightly cluttered, not dramatic.

**Landing 2: the transformation (8 s).** Same counter, same light. The bills lift gently off the heap and drift into neat alignment while thin hairlines of ink `#1D2421` and a few of vermilion `#BC3A22` draw between them, like threads being pulled tight. They glide toward a cloth-bound ledger lying open on the counter, ruled pages in cotton `#FBF8F0` with blank lines, and each bill flattens into one ruled row. Smooth, unhurried, precise. No text, no numbers, no faces.

**Landing 3: resolved, seamless loop (8 s).** Overhead, locked-off, then a barely perceptible drift that returns to the start pose. The open ledger shows a few clean, blank ruled rows. A brass-handled rubber stamp lifts, lands once on the last row with a soft thump, leaving an abstract vermilion `#BC3A22` ring-and-bars impression (no letters), and lifts away; the paper settles and the frame returns to where it began so the end matches the first frame. Cream `#F4EEE1` ground, leaf `#2F6B4F` appears only as a small pencil on the counter. Tranquil and finished.

## Sign-in / sign-up backdrops

The centre (sign-in) or the right half (sign-up) must stay **brighter and quieter**: keep detail and contrast to the edges.

**Sign-in 1 (8 s loop).** Static macro of a closed cloth-bound ledger and a brass key on a cream `#F4EEE1` counter, window light slowly moving across the cloth, a few dust motes drifting. Trustworthy, quiet, centre soft and empty.

**Sign-in 2 (8 s loop).** Slow dolly past a row of tidy shelves of bound ledgers in kraft `#EAE2D0` and ink `#1D2421`, shallow depth of field, the centre of frame a soft bright blur of window light. No faces.

**Sign-up 1 (8 s loop).** A shop doorway with a folding wooden shutter sliding open onto warm daylight, cream `#F4EEE1` interior, a small vermilion `#BC3A22` doormat the only accent. Welcoming, left half detailed, right half calm and bright.

**Sign-up 2 (8 s loop).** Two pairs of hands only (no faces) slide an open ledger across a shared wooden table, one hand setting a pen down on it, then the scene resets. Right half quiet cream `#FBF8F0`.

## After you add files

- Convert clips to `.webm` + `.mp4` with a `.jpg` poster each; logos and icons to `.webp`.
- Concatenate the three landing clips and extract about 200 to 260 frames:
  `ffmpeg -i landing.mp4 -vf "fps=10,scale=1600:-1" -c:v libwebp -quality 72 seq/f_%03d.webp` (target under about 12 MB total).
- I confirm the filename pattern and the frame count with you before coding the sequence.
